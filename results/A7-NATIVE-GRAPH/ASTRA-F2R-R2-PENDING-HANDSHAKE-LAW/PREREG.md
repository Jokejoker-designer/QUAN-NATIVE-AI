## Selected pending

On PICK, after **all** legal slots are scored:

```text
pending = {gen[7:0], txn[7:0], sel_idx[1:0], p0, p1, ans,
           phi[0:31] copied from scored bank[sel_idx], v_pred}

```

`sel_idx` is the enumerated slot, not reconstructed from proof0. Shared proof0 with different hop2/phi must copy that slot's scored phi.

## Reward handshake

Bus: `{rew_v, rew signed[3:0], rew_txn[7:0], rew_gen[7:0]}`.
On the **valid cycle only**, if pending accepted && !committed && SGD ready:

- rew_gen != pend_gen → n_stale, no update
- rew_txn != pend_txn → n_bad, no update
- rew not in [-3,+3] → n_oor, no update
- else latch rew into `rew_lat`, pulse SGD go_upd **next cycle** with reward=`rew_lat` and x=`pend_phi`

Next-cycle bus changes must not affect `rew_lat` or `pend_phi`.
Commit only after SGD done (all 32 writes). Then n_upd++.

Freeze (ctrl=1): handshake ignored for update; n_upd=0; not committed.
Zero reward: commit, n_upd++, dw=0 if v=0.

Duplicate after commit: n_dup, no second dw.
Retire in HOLD before upd: drop pending, no dw.
Retire during S_UW: **drain** — SGD finishes, then commit (no half-write).
Hard reset during pending/upd: clear weights and pending (abort, not silent half-commit).
